WHAT THIS IS

This prototype does not forecast the probability of a lockout. It shows how a specified event propagates through a dependency structure and where leverage or substitutes stop it.
Evidence & assumptions

France is the worked case. Structural examples use public relationships; the widths and recovery shares shown here are illustrative scenario settings for demonstration.

Primary references include the Middle Powers RFP, Mistral sovereign-compute announcements, and the Microsoft–Mistral partnership.

Technical method

SROAI separates the size of exposed activity from whether disruption attenuates or amplifies as it moves downstream. In the full method, propagation depends on exposure, within-window non-recoverability and fan-out. The visual intentionally translates those quantities into plain-language outcomes.
